## Machine notes
- **Plate:** ships with Textured PEI. Clean with **warm water + dish soap, NOT alcohol**.
- **When it complains about the plate:** re-run auto bed-leveling; if adhesion still off, wash the plate (5-sec water-sheet test) and handle by edges only.
- Scraper: use the **printed plastic blade** on textured PEI, not the metal one (metal is for flat glass).
